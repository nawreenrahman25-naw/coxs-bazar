Dear Faisal,

I don't know if this little surprise will make you smile, but I really hope it does.

We talked about it so many times, didn't we? Some plans changed, some got postponed, and some are still waiting to become real.

And I thought... maybe it's finally time to make one of those plans real.

I want to turn one of our old plans
into a beautiful memory.

So, Faisal...

Pack your bags. 🧳
Let's go to Cox's Bazar. 🌊

There's no fixed date because I want you to choose a time when you're comfortable and free. Whenever you're free, just let me know, and we'll plan it together.

I want us to stay there for 2 nights and 2 days— enough time to slow down, enjoy the sea, watch the sunset, eat good food, take pictures, laugh over random things, and simply enjoy the little escape.

And please don't worry about the expenses.

Everything will be my treat. 🤍

From my side, I also want to give you a small gift—something I hope you'll keep for a lifetime. Not because it's something expensive, but because I want it to remind you of this little adventure and this moment.

I know a trip is just a trip, but sometimes a simple trip can become one of those memories we remember for years.

So I wanted to ask you...

Whenever you're free,
will you let me know?

We'll choose our dates, pack our bags, and finally make one of those old plans happen.

Take your time, Faisal. There's no pressure. I just wanted to make this little invitation from my heart.

I'll be waiting for your reply. 💌

Cox's Bazar is waiting... and hopefully, one beautiful little adventure is waiting for us too.
